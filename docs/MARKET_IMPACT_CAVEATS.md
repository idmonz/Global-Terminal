# Market Impact Caveats

Goal 6 market impact outputs are informational and educational only.

## What The Engine Shows

- Related assets based on deterministic entity/keyword/region mappings.
- Potential market channels.
- Observed related market moves when real price history is supplied or fetched.
- Proxy reactions and scenario evidence weights.
- Data gaps when price, volume, estimate, or source fields are unavailable.

## What The Engine Does Not Claim

- It does not claim causality.
- It does not claim a trading edge.
- It does not provide personalized financial advice.
- It does not fill missing provider fields with fabricated data.

Always verify with primary source data.
