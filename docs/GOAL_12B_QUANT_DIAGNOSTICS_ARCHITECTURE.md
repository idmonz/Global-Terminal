# Goal 12B Quant Diagnostics Architecture

Options, option-chain, volatility-surface, option-book, and model outputs are research calculations only. They are not investment advice, trading recommendations, trade approvals, hedging instructions, or execution instructions.

Diagnostics use supplied local time series only: returns, rolling mean/volatility, correlation/eigenvalues, RMT-style caveat, EWMA volatility, volatility clustering, Hurst, OU half-life, and pair residual diagnostics. Heavy runtimes remain excluded.
