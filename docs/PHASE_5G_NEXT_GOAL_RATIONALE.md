# Phase 5G Next Goal Rationale

Updated: 2026-06-19

## Selected Next Phase

Phase 5H: Global Map Intelligence.

## Why

- The Market Home and AAPL/BTCUSDT journey now has usable MVP evidence.
- The map category remains 2/5 and is one of the user's clearest product objections.
- Leaflet, War Room, map-news-event fusion, USGS, GDELT/RSS, provider health, layer registry, timeline, and event-asset graph foundations already exist.
- A vertical map phase can convert those foundations into daily value without inventing another broad schema.

## Not Selected

- Screener/heatmap: useful, but map intelligence is the larger experience and parity gap.
- Options: still provider-gated and narrower in daily audience.
- Portfolio/research memory: important after discovery and global-risk surfaces are credible.

## Durable Goal Status

Do not mark the master benchmark-parity goal complete. Finviz, map, data realness, options, portfolio, research memory, and commercial platform categories remain below strong internal beta.
