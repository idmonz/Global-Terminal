# OpenBB Portfolio Quant Integration

Portfolio Quant Lab can request OpenBB-backed price history through source modes while keeping native price history available.

Goal 8 also adds Strategy Research Engine v2:

- `lib/strategy_backtest_engine.py`
- `lib/strategy_combination_engine.py`
- `lib/walk_forward_validator.py`
- `lib/oos_validation.py`
- `lib/strategy_correlation.py`
- `lib/strategy_report_cards.py`

Backtests and model-estimated forward distributions are for research only. They are not financial advice, not predictions, and not recommendations. Past performance does not guarantee future results.
