## Native Phase 5U Adoption

The adopted behavior is first-party and project-native:

- compact source boundary;
- map-before-replay layout;
- visible layer rows and event inspector;
- source/gap honesty;
- related reporting and asset transmission where the contract already supplies them;
- visual regression harness for actual map position and nonblank state.

Outputs remain research calculations and information displays only. They are not investment advice, recommendations, trade approvals, broker actions, autonomous monitoring, or execution instructions.
