# Phase 5L Next Goal Rationale

Updated: 2026-06-22

## Decision

The next single vertical slice is Phase 5M Universal Symbol Coverage And Search.

## Why

Research Memory now gives the product continuity, but most discovered/watchlisted instruments still cannot open a verified workstation. That blocks TradingView-style daily usefulness, Finviz discovery-to-detail conversion and OpenBB-style source-aware breadth more than another isolated panel would.

## Required Outcome

Search and resolve a bounded free-first equity/ETF/crypto universe, open source-aware historical charts and available fundamentals for supported symbols, retain interval/range state, flow from Market Map/watchlists into the workstation, and preserve explicit unsupported/provider-gap semantics.

## Not Yet

Hosted auth, billing, broker integration, recommendation generation, paid provider lock-in and broad portfolio ingestion remain later commercial-platform work.
